## 摘要
在多基地雷达系统中，多个接收机协同工作以提高目标定位精度，该系统在协同同步定位与建图（SLAM）以及自主机器人网络等领域有着广泛应用。这些应用中的一个关键挑战是，由于平台移动性导致的雷达接收机位置和姿态（位姿）的不确定性。本文通过推导多基地雷达系统进行双基地距离和方位测量时的克拉美-罗下界（CRLB），研究了目标定位和接收机位姿估计的可实现改进。我们提出了一种交替加权最小二乘算法，该算法联合优化目标和接收机参数。蒙特卡洛仿真表明，在低到中等噪声水平下，该算法的性能接近CRLB。

## Abstract
Target localization in a multistatic radar system, where multiple receivers cooperate to improve target positioning accuracy, has many applications, including cooperative simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM) and autonomous robot networks. A key challenge in these applications is the uncertainty in the position and orientation (pose) of the radar receivers due to platform mobility. This work investigates the achievable improvements in both target localization and receiver pose estimation by deriving the Cramer-Rao lower bound (CRLB) for a multistatic radar system performing bistatic range and bearing measurements. We propose an alternating weighted least-squares algorithm that jointly optimizes target and receiver parameters. Monte Carlo simulations demonstrate that the algorithm performance approaches the CRLB for low to moderate noise levels.
