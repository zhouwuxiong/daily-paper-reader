## 摘要
360°全景图之间的稠密特征匹配支撑了全向位姿估计、三维重建和SLAM。此类全景图以等距柱状投影（ERP）存储，该投影将观察球面展开到平面图上，从而引入三种不同的畸变——纵向接缝（拓扑）、随纬度变化的拉伸（度量）和非均匀像素面积（面积）——而透视图像训练的稠密匹配器的粗阶段并未对其建模，因此这些匹配器在ERP上会系统性地退化。我们表明，在粗阶段产生这些畸变的接口处进行校正——注意力中的成对畸变、共视门控中的逐像素畸变——可在固定粗匹配骨架且细化器架构不变的情况下，将Matterport3D上的PCK@1°从0.230提升到0.275——这是我们的核心结果。具体而言，SCCM（球面一致性粗匹配）用两种由球面导出的先验增强了一个未考虑图面畸变的交叉注意力/双softmax粗匹配器：球面位置注意力（SPA）将偏航周期性的RoPE（拓扑）与切平面偏置（度量）相结合，面积感知共视（AAC）则应用sigmoid前的对数面积校正（面积）。未考虑图面畸变的骨架作为受控参照，将骨架替换效应与球面先验效应分离开来。SCCM在RoMa V1框架中实例化，使用相同的冻结编码器、细化器架构和损失，在统一的ERP稠密匹配协议下也优于ERP原生EDM（0.163）和在ERP上重新训练的RoMa V1（0.198），而透视训练的匹配器在ERP上大多失败。它还可零样本迁移到Stanford2D3D，并且在户外Holo360D上训练后，在该数据集上也领先。

## Abstract
Dense feature matching between 360$^\circ$ panoramas underpins omnidirectional pose estimation, 3D reconstruction, and SLAM. Such panoramas are stored in the equirectangular projection (ERP), which unrolls the viewing sphere onto a flat chart and thereby introduces three distinct distortions -- a longitudinal seam (topology), latitude-dependent stretch (metric), and non-uniform pixel area (area) -- that the coarse stage of perspective-trained dense matchers does not model, so these matchers degrade systematically on ERP. We show that correcting the three distortions at the coarse-stage interfaces where they arise -- pairwise distortions in attention, per-pixel distortion in covisibility gating -- improves PCK@1$^\circ$ from 0.230 to 0.275 on Matterport3D under a fixed coarse scaffold, with the refiner architecture unchanged -- our central result. Concretely, SCCM (Spherically Consistent Coarse Matching) augments a chart-naive cross-attention/dual-softmax coarse matcher with two sphere-derived priors: Spherical Positional Attention (SPA) pairs a yaw-periodic RoPE (topology) with a tangent-plane bias (metric), and Area-Aware Covisibility (AAC) applies a pre-sigmoid log-area correction (area). The chart-naive scaffold serves as a controlled reference, separating the scaffold-replacement effect from the spherical-prior effect. Instantiated in the RoMa V1 framework with the same frozen encoder, refiner architecture, and loss, SCCM also outperforms the ERP-native EDM (0.163) and an ERP-retrained RoMa V1 (0.198) under a unified ERP dense matching protocol, while perspective-trained matchers largely fail on ERP. It further transfers zero-shot to Stanford2D3D and, when trained on outdoor Holo360D, leads there as well.
